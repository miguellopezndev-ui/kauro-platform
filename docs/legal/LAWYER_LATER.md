# D-08b — questions for a lawyer later, and owner fill-ins

Owner decision D-08b (2026-10-05): no external lawyer until there is revenue or a contract/customer that justifies one. These are
the residual uncertainties that a qualified reviewer should settle then. Each says why it matters, what the texts do today, and
the risk if the current choice is wrong. Ordered by risk.

## Legal questions

| ID | Question | Why it matters | Current choice in the texts | Risk if wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-01 | Do the providers' standard contracts (AWS Service Terms + AWS DPA, Resend DPA, Google Workspace terms) satisfy the Colombian **contrato de transmisión** (Decreto 1074 art. 2.2.2.25.5.2) and the CCPA sub-processor flow-down (§1798.140(ag)(2), 11 CCR §7051(b))? | Kauro cannot negotiate these contracts; they do not mention Colombian law. Kauro's DPA §8.1 promises equivalent terms down the chain | Treated as sufficient, with the US on the SIC adequacy list (CHECKLIST C-21, C-22, C-63) | Medium: an SIC finding that the transmission lacks a qualifying contract, or a customer claim that the flow-down promise in DPA §8.1 is unmet |
| L-02 | How far does Ley 1581 reach when Kauro is the processor of a **US controller's** data about **US residents**? | Determines whether the SIC incident report (DPA §11.3) and Colombian processor duties apply to that data, and which law a US data subject could invoke | Conservative: Kauro applies Ley 1581 processor duties to all Customer Data and reports incidents to the SIC | Low for compliance (over-complying), but reporting US-only incidents to the SIC could create avoidable exposure or conflict with the customer's own notice plan |
| L-03 | Lawful basis for **B2B prospecting** with business contact data of agency staff taken from public websites (no prior authorization) | Decreto 1074 art. 2.2.2.25.2.2 allows public-source data only "si, por su naturaleza, son datos públicos"; a work email is arguably not public by nature. Recipients are mostly US residents (CAN-SPAM allows opt-out email) | POL §6.3 and §8.2: limited purpose, source and date recorded, immediate opt-out, agencies only | Medium: SIC could consider the prospect database processed without authorization; fines under Ley 1581 art. 23 |
| L-04 | Is the **liability cap** (fees paid in the prior 12 months, carve-outs for fraud, gross negligence, price, non-waivable rights and role liability) enforceable and adequate? Should data-protection breaches have a separate higher cap? | Código Civil arts. 1522 and 1616 allow modifying liability except for fraud (and gross negligence by art. 63); US agencies often expect a data-breach super-cap | ToS §18 | Medium: a court could void the cap in a data incident, or US customers may refuse the terms |
| L-05 | **Forum and enforceability** against US customers: Colombian courts at Kauro's domicile, after 30 days of negotiation; is arbitration (e.g. a Colombian chamber of commerce, in English) better for US buyers? | A Colombian judgment must be recognized (exequatur-like process) in a US state to collect | ToS §22 | Medium: practically unenforceable against a non-paying US customer; customers may object to a foreign court |
| L-06 | Is an **in-house team's company** a "consumidor" under Ley 1480 art. 5 num. 3 (need not intrinsically linked to its economic activity)? If yes: retracto (art. 47, modified by Ley 2439 de 2024), reversión del pago (art. 51), e-commerce duties (art. 50), abusive-clause review (arts. 42–43) | A3 admits in-house buyers on the same flow; agencies are clearly not consumers | ToS keeps non-waivable rights (§11.3, §17.2, §18.3(c)) and refunds 100 % before processing (§10.1) | Low–medium: a Colombian in-house buyer could claim retracto or that the cap is abusive; most in-house buyers will be US companies outside Ley 1480 |
| L-07 | **VAT and withholding** treatment of the service to US customers (export of services, ET art. 481 lit. c; DIAN Concepto 5846 de 2024) and to Colombian customers | Price shown "before taxes"; the Quote must show the right total | ToS §7.5 neutral; no exemption promised (accountant question, also in 19 §Specific unresolved application questions) | High for money, low for the text: wrong VAT means Kauro owes the tax out of its margin |
| L-08 | Current text of the **Colorado Privacy Act** 6-1-1305 and other state laws (Texas, Connecticut, Oregon, etc.) for processor terms added after 2021 | Only the 2021 enacted Colorado text and Virginia §59.1-579 were read | DPA §14 written to the common VA/CO/CA terms | Low: most state laws share these terms; a missed newer requirement would surface as a customer redline |
| L-09 | Whether to offer **GDPR/UK GDPR** processor terms and SCCs (EU/UK residents in US agencies' CRMs) | GDPR art. 3(2) and art. 28 may reach a non-EU processor via the controller; authority 19 says it depends on roles and geography | Not offered; prior evaluation required (ToS §5.5) | Medium: agencies with EU contacts may upload anyway; the exclusion clause shifts responsibility but not all risk |
| L-10 | Are the **retention periods for Kauro's own data** (POL §15: accounts 6 months after closure, prospects 24 months, leads 12 months, requests 5 years) reasonable under Decreto 1074 art. 2.2.2.25.2.8, and is 10 years right for all financial records? | Authority 09 fixes only CRM, log, security and financial classes | Builder proposals | Low |
| L-11 | **Language**: Spanish prevails for ToS and POL, both versions of the DPA are equal. US customers use an English product | A US customer accepting a Spanish-only contract can argue lack of informed consent | ToS §24.4; English ToS/POL translations needed before the first US sale | Medium: contract formation dispute with a US customer |
| L-12 | **Seller (independent sales collaborator) relationship**: classification, commercial-agency implications under the Código de Comercio, and whether sellers who see agency contact data are processors needing a transmission contract | Authority 17 §Legal engagement packet already flags classification; R2-110 | Sellers get no CRM data (DPA §8.3); POL §6.4 covers their own data | Medium (labor/commercial), outside these customer texts |

Code §1798.82(b), not read: CHECKLIST C-67)? | Customers may need faster notice in their own contracts | DPA §11.1: without undue delay and within 72 h | Low |
