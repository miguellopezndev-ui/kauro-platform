# D-08b — questions for a lawyer later, and owner fill-ins

Owner decision D-08b (2026-10-05): no external lawyer until there is revenue or a contract/customer that justifies one. These are
the residual uncertainties that a qualified reviewer should settle then. Each says why it matters, what the texts do today, and
the risk if the current choice is wrong. Ordered by risk. Rows marked **Closed (r2)** were material defects found by the Codex
r1 review (2026-10-06) and are fixed in the texts now; they stay listed so the history is visible.
