lawyer, checked against official sources, adversarial review by Codex afterwards, owner's written acceptance before B19).

Files covered: `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` (ToS), `DATA_PROCESSING_POLICY.md` (POL), `PRIVACY_NOTICE.md` (NOTICE), `DPA.md` (DPA), `INTERNAL_DATA_MANUAL.md` (MAN, internal), `PIA_BEDROCK.md` (PIA, internal), `LAWYER_LATER.md`. All texts are English masters (owner rule 2026-10-07: customers are US companies); Spanish secondary translations of the habeas-data materials (POL, NOTICE, MAN) are kept only in `es/`, reachable only through the language selector. Every source below was consulted on **2026-10-06** unless stated otherwise. Revision **r2** (same day) fixes the 14 findings of
the Codex adversarial review r1 of commit 9f7ab66 (9 MAJOR, 5 MINOR) under the coordinator's rulings; each changed row says r2.
Revision **r3** fixes the Codex r2 review of 1d653e1 (4 partially fixed findings and 2 new MINORs); changed rows say r3.
Revision **r4** fixes the Codex r3 review of 936bafd (F-07 payment reversal, F-17 consumer pricing, F-18 state coverage).

Status values:
- **COVERED**: the obligation is met by the cited clause.
- **COVERED_IF_BUILT**: the clause is correct only if the build/configuration it describes is in place before activation; the
  activation check is named.
- **OPEN**: a non-text artifact or action is still missing; it blocks B19 activation until done.
- **N/A**: the norm does not apply to Kauro's actual scope; the reason is stated.
- **UNVERIFIED**: the official text could not be retrieved; the clause does not rely on it.
