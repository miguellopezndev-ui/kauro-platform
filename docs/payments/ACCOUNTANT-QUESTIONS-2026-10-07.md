# Questions for the accountant: collecting from US customers through Wise (deliverable 9c, Payments V1)

English master. A Spanish copy for the meeting is kept at `es/ACCOUNTANT-QUESTIONS-2026-10-07.es.md` (secondary; this file
prevails if they differ).

Context for the accountant: KAURO DATA S.A.S. (Colombia) will sell software / data-migration services to US companies,
invoiced in USD. The customer pays by ACH or wire to the USD account details of a **Wise Business** account in the company's
name (Wise is a foreign entity; the USD details belong to a US bank partnered with Wise). The company then keeps USD in Wise
and/or converts and brings them to its account in Colombia (Bancolombia business account). There are no subscriptions:
billing is per project. Some projects may end in a full or partial refund to the customer.

## A. Foreign-exchange regime (Banco de la República)
1. Does receiving service-export payments in a Wise account (abroad) require registering it as a **compensation account
   (cuenta de compensación)** with Banco de la República? Or can it be channelled another way (e.g. monetised through an IMC)?
2. If it is a compensation account: which periodic reports and exchange declarations (forms) are required, how often and
   with which deadlines? Who files them and which supporting documents does Kauro need to provide (Wise statements,
   invoices, receipts)?
3. Is a service export a mandatory-channelling or a free-market operation? Does the answer change if the USD stay in Wise
   for some time before being brought to Colombia?
4. To bring the USD to Colombia: is it better to convert to COP inside Wise and send COP to Bancolombia, or to send USD to an
   account in Colombia and monetise here? Which one complies better with the regime and leaves better support documents?
5. Are there limits, penalties or risks (e.g. non-channelled operations) we must avoid from the first collection?
6. A **refund** to the US customer (USD leaving Wise): how is it reported for exchange purposes? Which support do we need
   (invoice, credit note, Wise receipt)?

## B. Tax (DIAN)
7. Do our services to US customers qualify as a **service export** exempt from VAT (IVA)? Which requirements and supporting
   documents apply (contract or equivalent document, invoice, proof of payment, use of the service abroad)?
8. **Electronic invoicing**: how do we invoice in USD to a foreign customer (currency, exchange rate, buyer data without a
   Colombian NIT)? Which provider/software and which fields are mandatory?
9. Which exchange rate do we use to record the income: the invoice date's, the payment date's, or the monetisation date's?
   How is the exchange difference treated?
10. Are Wise fees (e.g. USD 6.11 per incoming wire, the COP conversion cost) deductible expenses? Which support is valid
    (the Wise statement in English)?
11. A full or partial **refund**: is an electronic credit note issued? How does it affect VAT (if applicable), income and
    withholding, and in which period?
12. Is there **withholding tax** in the US or in Colombia on these collections? Do we need to give the US customer forms
    (e.g. W-8BEN-E) to avoid withholding there?
13. Must we report the Wise account in the exógena (information return) / foreign assets? From which balance?

## C. Operation and support documents Kauro will keep (to confirm)
14. For each collection Kauro will keep: invoice, unique payment reference, Wise statement/receipt of the credit received,
    and the reconciliation in Kauro. For each refund: authorisation, Wise outgoing receipt, credit note. Is anything missing
    for foreign-exchange or DIAN purposes?
15. Is there any requirement for the **first** test operation (a small real collection and its refund) that we must meet so
    that it is recorded correctly?

The accountant's answers are integrated into B09/B15 (income, refund and support-document records) and into the legal texts
(D-08b). None of the accountant's answers is replaced by an assumption of ours.
