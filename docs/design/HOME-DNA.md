# Home DNA (round 3 foundations; a blurred sketch, measured)

What "like Home" means for every other page and document: a prior and a vocabulary, not a quota (master plan §4). Every page decides its
own amounts in its brief and auditions.

### 1.2 The shape down the page (1280, 6,132 px tall)
1. **Warm opening (0–800 px).** The hero's rock: brown and sand together reach 11–15% of each strip.
2. **Calm middle (800–3,900 px).** Carbon at 91–100% per strip. The content is the product's own fragments, light and grey on carbon.
3. **Wine climax (about 65% of the way down).** A wine object at about 4,000 px, then a full wine band of about 600 px at 85–98% wine.
4. **Warm close.** The closing ticket on rock (brown up to 46%), then the footer on carbon.

The pattern to carry, blurred: **warm opening, calm middle, one wine climax around two-thirds down, warm close.** Wine is rare
(one climax, about a tenth of the page) and therefore loud. Sand and clay are the action colour and the light on the rock. Brown is the
rock's shadow and the accent surface.

## 2. Type (as measured; the kit will define the real scale)
- **26 distinct font sizes in use.** About 16 are steps chosen by design (11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 30, 38,
  42 px). The rest are fluid display sizes (about 38–84 px, `clamp`) and scaled miniatures inside the objects.
- **6 weights:** 300, 400, 500, 600, 650, 700. The light 300 carries the two-weight headline's accent words.
- **Roles seen:** a display headline in two weights (heavy, then light accent words); eyebrows in small capitals with tracking; section
  headings at 42 px (1280); body at 15–17 px; meta at 13–14 px; miniatures at 11–12 px.

## 3. Space
- **40 distinct spacing values in use.** The frequent ones: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48, 96, and a second family on 5, 6, 10, 14, 20.
- The kit will settle on one scale. Home's outliers are inside the tilted objects, where they are part of the illustration.

## 4. Objects, textures, motion (the vocabulary)
- **Objects:** paper objects (piles, folders, tickets, stamps, windows of the product) on the brand's rock textures (clay, ember, wine,
  carbon). They are tilted 1–3°, with deep soft shadows.
- **Textures** only ever sit under an object.
- **Motion:** pointer reactions only.
  - A card straightens and lifts 4 px (0.45 s, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1)`).
  - The pile fans out.
  - The stamp "thunks" on arrival.

  Nothing moves on its own or with the scroll (owner, Order 10). Reduced motion keeps everything still.

## 5. Home's own audit (it is not exempt)

At 1280:
- **25 text nodes under 12 px**, inside the product miniatures. They are decorative fragments; whether they are hidden from assistive
  technology is to be confirmed.
- **4 targets 22 px tall** (the pile's tabs: Report, Cleaned files, Manifest, Import steps). They are under the 24 px minimum.
- **14 left-edge near-misses**, mostly inside tilted objects, where they are expected.

These are recorded findings for Home's own pass (§10.4 of the master plan). They do not change its look.

## 6. What carries to the private sites and documents
Each private site derives its own DNA in Foundations step 8 (vertical slice), from this one and from the system research. Open for the
slice to decide with evidence:
- how much rock and wine a tool screen can carry;
- the tool tier of the type scale;
- whether the wine climax has an equivalent in a tool (for example, the one decision that needs the owner).

Documents use the light print theme (deliverables plan §1.2) with the same vocabulary.
