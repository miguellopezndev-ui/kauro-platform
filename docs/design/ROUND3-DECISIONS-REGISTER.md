## 0. How to read this register

**Status words**
- **Binding**: a hard limit, a contract in force (security, accessibility, copy A3, registry, legal texts), the law, or an owner
  instruction about process. It changes only by the owner or by its own procedure.
- **Binding (owner)**: an owner business or product decision. Under D-34 it may be replaced only by the owner, after a proposal with its
  effects; round 3 designs to it.
- **Evidence (D-34)**: an earlier design decision, approved or not. It is a strong prior and a transition constraint, never a veto: an
  alternative that wins its audition clearly (master plan §9.4) replaces it, with a record of what changed and why.
- **Open**: waiting for the owner or counsel. Design must not present it as settled.
- **Proposed**: drawn in the review, not adopted into a contract; needs the owner and a functional change.
- **Superseded by X**: no longer governs; kept so it is not revived by mistake.
