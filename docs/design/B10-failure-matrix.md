# B10 failure-mode matrix

Status: test inventory at `b10/failure-matrix` (from `b10/stage-3` d0ee96a, all stage 1 to 3 lanes merged), revision r1 after Codex's design-time review. Documentation only; no product code.
Template and column definitions: `docs/design/FAILURE-MATRIX-TEMPLATE.md`. Fault harness: `docs/design/FAULT-HARNESS.md` (built separately).
Design source: `docs/design/B10-commercial-domain.md` (decisions D10-*).

How to read a cell. Every cell starts with its status.
- **C** covered: the expected behaviour, then `[key]` naming the proving test; the test index at the end maps each key to `file :: exact title`.
- **GAP** `<code>`: not proven by any existing test; the text is the behaviour the design implies; the code names the entry in the GAP list at the end, which holds the proposed test title.
- **N/A**: the reason the failure mode cannot occur in that step.

Counting rule. A cell is **C** only if an existing test title asserts that behaviour. A retry test proves the replay of a committed answer, so the "crash after COMMIT" column of a single-transaction command is a GAP (code CMD_CRA / PAY_CRA / CLOSE_CRA / CONS_CRA) until the harness injects the loss. "Crash before COMMIT" is covered only where the cited test dies after the step's last write and before its own COMMIT. Revision r1 (after Codex's design-time review, `Codex r1 findings` at the end) applies the family rules of the template: sequential calls never prove a race; a guard or database invariant is a DENY or OK test, never a PARTIAL cell (PARTIAL needs a failure injected at a later write); every external effect with more than one durable write has one row per write. Database-level tests (`... at the database`) are cited only for single-statement steps, as N/A reasons.

## CMD027 included-replacement protocol

Paid project, entitlement ACTIVE, allowance unspent (D10-QUOTE-3, D10-QUOTE-4, R2-092): intent (tenant COMMIT: replacement ISSUED, quote_issuances, journal entry RESERVED) then settle (SERVICE unit of work, PurchaseRebinder, one COMMIT: re-binding recorded with its allowance ordinal, old entitlement REVOKED SOURCE_REPLACED, replaced quote SUPERSEDED, replacement ACCEPTED_FOR_CHECKOUT, new entitlement issued under the re-binding) then complete (tenant COMMIT: journal COMMITTED with the answer; D10-QUOTE-6). A refused re-binding writes nothing in the SERVICE transaction and is followed by the compensation row S2r (withdraw the replacement, complete the journal with outcome WITHDRAWN). The old entitlement is revoked and the allowance spent by S2, not by the tenant completion S3. Tier 2 = ledger and delivery integrity that does not by itself change a payable right (a sweep timing out holds rather than releases, a duplicated period open, a stranded delivery, a source deadline). Tier 3 = availability and operability (the wrong outcome is a retriable error or a lost response). Within tier 1 the order is by money impact (payout and cash, then statements and corrections, then accrual, conversion and holds, then replacement and quote, then attribution and authority); within tiers 2 and 3 by number of cells. Each proposed title follows the existing convention (`RACE ...`, `DENY ...`) and is the test-first input of B10-INTEGRATION and of the Codex review. A cell is closed by a test that uses the matching harness facility; a sequential call, a guard or a database invariant does not close a RACE, CRASH or PARTIAL cell.

## Rulings R-GAP-1 to R-GAP-3 (2026-10-04, under owner delegation; revocable)

- **R-GAP-1** (`PAY_SETTLE_FLIP` visible case): B10 adds no payout-contradiction case kind; the registry has none and inventing one is a registry change. The contradiction is visible where the evidence comes from: the second evidence is refused 409 UNLISTED_TRANSITION naming the intent's state (D10-GAP-5), and the PayoutEvidenceVerifier/evidence adapter (B15) must turn that refusal into an operator alert. Recorded as a B15 contract requirement; the B10 cell stays covered by `[gflip]`.
- **R-GAP-2** (`CLOSE_VS_DELIVERY`): the statement input is defined by creation time, not occurred_at (D10-STMT-4): the close's cutoff instant is the unit's server instant; every COMMISSIONS record created before it must be delivered or the close is refused INPUTS_UNRECONCILED (adapter undeliveredBefore at that instant, checked before the proposal and again on the proposal recomputed under the line locks), every line existing when the close reads under its period and line locks is in the statement, and the cutoff hash refuses a close whose inputs changed under it (STALE_VERSION LEDGER_CHANGED). Every COMMISSIONS delivery takes the open periods FOR SHARE (D10-GAP-10), so no delivery commits between the close's checks and its COMMIT: a record committed after the close's read is delivered after the close and its line is the next statement's input even if it occurred inside the period, so no line is omitted from both. Corrected twice on 2026-10-04: after GAP lane 2 (the boundary is the close's cutoff instant, not the period end) and after Codex r1 B10G-R5 (the code used period.endsAt and the proposal's count was not enforced; both fixed). Covered by the race cell `[gvsrace]` and the DENY test `[gvsdelivery]`.
- **R-GAP-3** (`CORR_APPROVE`, `CORR_DUP`, `CORR_PAR` and the K1/K2 rows): the ISSUED/DISPUTED to CORRECTED edge on correction_issued is declared by the accepted B10 design, not by the authority registry: the registry (`state_transitions.json`) has a CommissionLine machine and no CommissionStatement machine, and its CMD051 (`commands.json`) declares only the period-close route and its `ledgerCutoffHash` body. The edge is in the platform-machine table of `B10-commercial-domain.md` (section 8.2, CommissionStatement, performer CMD051) and seeded by migration 0031, so it is B10 scope as accepted design, and building it is a design decision (D10-GAP-7), not a registry mandate. Performer and invocation, as the accepted matrix resolution B10-M3 leaves open and this ruling settles: in B10 the performer is the commission service identity (role COMMISSION_SERVICE, checked by the service pipeline before the unit opens), invoked in process as the application operation `correctCommissionStatement` under the journal operation CMD051, no route; the owner's reviewed recovery (Q10-6) is not built in B10, and when it is it must invoke the same operation under the KAURO_OWNER grant and a fresh step-up judged at the effect. `CORR_AUTH` covers both performers. Built in B10 GAP lane 2, tests first, with the cells' proposed tests as acceptance: one successor per predecessor, APPROVED never corrected, no intent against a CORRECTED statement, all-or-nothing successor plus predecessor transition. ## Family check after Codex r1 (directive A.4; written note, not built)

B10G-R4 is the second finding of the family "a cell passes without its fault" (a vacuous cell). The cell runner already refuses a cell whose kind lacks its evidence (`assertInjected`: a lock wait observed, a deadline crossed and consumed, a crash fired at the named boundary, a repeated attempt, a revocation then a fenced operation, a failure after a completed step), and every fault kind has a rule. What it cannot see is evidence the cell body manufactures beside the code under test: in r1 the clock crossed the timeout's deadline before the reconciliation started and a test-made verifier consumed the instant. The cheap automatic check proposed is a timeout rule in `assertInjected`: the move that crosses a timeout cell's deadline must happen while an operation of the cell is in flight (started before the move, settled after it), so a timeout always happens to something waiting. It is not built here because the harness's own self-tests (`packages/persistence/test/fault-harness.test.ts`, nine timeout cells) move the clock before their operations and would each need rework, which is not cheap; the B10 timeout cell already satisfies it (the clock crosses the bound inside the running reconciliation, and a mutation removing the application's bound fails the cell). Proposed for the harness package's next change.
