# Owner decision — Payments V1 (2026-10-07)

Status: owner decision of 2026-10-07 (chat instruction, recorded by the builder in substance; no amendment number is assigned
because the amendment scheme does not require one for an owner decision that narrows launch scope). Where it contradicts an
earlier document, this decision wins. Earlier text is kept and marked SUPERSEDED, never deleted.

## Supersedes
- **A8** (`docs/authority/amendments/A8_PAYMENT_ROUTING.md`) in what concerns PayPal Business as the secondary/fallback route
  of the launch (rank 2; rule 2 "launches with PayPal only"; rule 3 "Alternative: PayPal / card"; the B19 gate "may activate
  with PayPal alone"; PayPal owner acts). The generic route model, project-based billing, currencies, "fees are data" and
  "optimise routing" rules stand.
- **D-15** in what is PayPal-specific (the 10 s PayPal provider-clock value). The GENERIC rule stays mandatory for every rail,

- **D-23** (PayPal support ticket): cancelled; superseded.

## 1. PayPal: out of the launch, PARKED (not deleted)
- Not done now: personal-account recovery, PayPal Business creation, support tickets, OAuth/credentials, webhooks, live
  Orders/Capture, PayPal-specific disputes, live integration, qualification, provider-specific tests, or any work that exists
  only to enable PayPal. Zero quota, time, CI or money on PayPal without a new owner authorisation.
- Code already built (PR #19 of S-B, branch `b15/adapters-paypal`) is neither deleted nor merged: it stays on its branch as a
  documented contingency (state DEFERRED). S-B closes it with a status commit and does no further work on it.
- PayPal returns only by an explicit owner decision; it is never activated automatically.

## 2. V1 target rail
Customer -> USD invoice -> ACH (or wire, same details) -> Wise Business USD account details of KAURO DATA S.A.S. ->
reconciliation in Kauro. Deel is ruled out (does not fit this sales model). Owner-verified fact: Wise Business admits
companies registered in Colombia.
The capability is NOT confirmed until all hold: (a) KAURO DATA S.A.S. registered; (b) Wise Business approved; (c) USD
details actually enabled; (d) ACH receiving actually available; (e) a real controlled collection AND refund test;
(f) correct reconciliation and evidence.
If Wise does not enable ACH/USD in time, PayPal is NOT reintroduced: the builder escalates to the owner with cause, date,
impact, real alternatives and one recommendation. Nothing wire-specific is built: the invoice shows the same USD details for
ACH and wire.

## 3. Kauro does not become Wise-specific
Generic abstractions are preserved: payment rail, payment evidence, reconciliation, refund, reversal, ledger, seller
commission consequences, fiscal consequences, receipt/entitlement. No generic contract is replaced by a Wise-coupled model;
PayPal, Stripe (if a legal route exists) or another B2B rail can be added later without rebuilding domain, ledger or refunds.

## 4. Refunds remain mandatory
V1 proves a real, controlled refund on the operating rail. For ACH/Wise it may be a safe manual-assisted flow provided: an
authorisation exists; the refund is recorded in Kauro idempotently; revenue/cash, the seller commission and the fiscal
evidence are adjusted when applicable; the receipt is kept; "refunded" is never set without real execution evidence; a double
refund cannot exist.
Rules common to every rail: an uncertain refund is NEVER re-sent, only queried; what stays uncertain is frozen for the owner's
manual resolution with evidence (D-24 A); a test fails if any path re-sends an uncertain refund.
No deep Wise API integration is built before launch if the safe manual flow meets the contract and the real tests.

## 5. Commercial scope
PRIMARY TARGET: ACH/USD (and wire) through Wise Business, conditioned on approval and real capability.
PAYPAL: post-launch / deferred / requires a new owner authorisation.
Kauro does not state publicly that it accepts ACH until proven on the real account.
Payment experience (S-E, B11): ACH takes 1-3 business days; define when work is admitted (only with confirmed funds), how the
quote expires while payment arrives, and what the agency sees meanwhile.

## 6. Construction
Recompute dependencies and gates of B10, B14-B19 and qualification. Generic tests of collection, payment evidence,
reconciliation, refunds, reversals, ledger, seller compensation, failure/retry and idempotency are never removed. Only what is
exclusive to PayPal is removed or deferred. A test mixing "payment correctness" with "PayPal correctness" is split into a
mandatory generic contract and a deferred PayPal adapter part.

## 7. Deliverables before 2026-10-10
(a) Official Wise fees for receiving USD by ACH and by wire, and for converting/sending to COP, with URL and date (no
third-party or AI figures). (b) Plan of the real ACH test: who in the US pays, what amount, and the controlled refund (if no
such payer exists: a blocker with alternatives). (c) Questions for the accountant (opening balance meeting): how KAURO DATA
S.A.S. receives and monetises US customer payments through Wise under the exchange regime (Banco de la Republica) and the
export-of-services treatment before the DIAN (VAT, electronic invoicing, supporting documents); the answers are integrated
into B09/B15 and the legal texts. (d) Notice to S-B and S-E through docs/b-requests on 2026-10-07.

## 8. Final rule
Reduce friction before launch and reach production and real cash sooner: do not rebuild payments or create new financial
complexity; do not delay production for PayPal; do not assume Wise until proven; do not sacrifice refunds, ledger,
reconciliation or monetary exactness. Savings are not converted into new scope.
